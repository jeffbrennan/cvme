# Task: tailor a cover letter

Produce a version of the BASE DOCUMENT below, addressed to the JOB POSTING.

Write the result to: `{output_path}`

## Constraints

- Keep the frontmatter keys, and fill `recipient`, `subject` and `date` from
  the posting where it says enough to do so. Leave a key out rather than
  guessing at a hiring manager's name.
- Between three and five paragraphs, and no more than {max_pages} page(s).
- Open with the specific thing about this role that matches something in the
  corpus. Not a greeting about the company's mission.
- Take one or two requirements from the posting and answer each with a
  concrete piece of work: what the problem was, what was built, what changed.
- If a requirement cannot be answered honestly from the corpus, leave it out.
  A shorter letter is better than a padded one.
- Close by saying what you would want to talk about. Not that you look forward
  to hearing back.
