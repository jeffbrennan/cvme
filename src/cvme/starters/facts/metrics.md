# Metrics

Every number you are entitled to claim. Write them exactly as you would want
them to appear, including any approximation.

`cvme verify` extracts every number from a generated document and requires it
to match something here, in skills.md, or in your base resume. A number that
appears nowhere in this corpus fails the build. If you want to claim "~$100k
per month", write "~$100k per month" here rather than "$98k" and rounding up
in the output.

- [m-example] Managed a platform at roughly $Nk per month in spend.
- [m-throughput] Processed N billion records per week.
- [m-team] Led a team of N engineers.
